# Data retention

Customer records shall be retained for no longer than 6 years after the account
is closed. Payment card numbers shall not be stored in any log.

Backups must be encrypted with AES-256 and must be tested at least once every
90 days. A backup that fails its test must be reported to the security team
within 24 hours.

Deletion requests must be completed within 30 days, unless the record is subject
to a legal hold.
