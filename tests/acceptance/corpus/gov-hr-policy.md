Annual Leave Policy

All employees are entitled to 25 days of annual leave per annum, in addition to public holidays. Leave entitlement is calculated on a pro rata basis for part-time employees.

Requests for leave must be submitted via the HR portal no later than 4 weeks in advance. Managers shall endeavour to approve requests in a timely manner, subject to operational requirements.

Up to 5 days of unused leave may be carried over into the following leave year. Any leave in excess of this amount will be forfeited unless exceptional circumstances apply.
