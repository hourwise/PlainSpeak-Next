The Tenant shall not assign, sublet or part with possession of the Property or any part thereof without the prior written consent of the Landlord, such consent not to be unreasonably withheld.

The Tenant shall pay the Rent of £1,250 per calendar month in advance on the first day of each month. If any Rent remains unpaid 14 days after the due date, interest shall be payable at 3% above the Bank of England base rate.

The Landlord shall be entitled to enter the Property upon giving not less than 24 hours' written notice, save in the case of emergency.
