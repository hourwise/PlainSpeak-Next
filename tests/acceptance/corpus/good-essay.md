My grandmother kept her recipes in a biscuit tin. None of them had quantities. "A handful of flour," one said, "and enough milk." For years I thought she was being mysterious on purpose.

She wasn't. She simply cooked by feel, the way you ride a bicycle without thinking about balance. When I finally made her scones without the tin, I understood: the recipe had never been the point. The point was to have made them so many times that you no longer needed to be told.
