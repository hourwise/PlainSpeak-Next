Section 4: Exclusions

We will not pay any claim arising from wear and tear, gradual deterioration or damage caused by vermin.

The policy excess is £250 for each claim. If you make more than two claims in any 12-month period, the excess for each subsequent claim will be £500.

Cover for valuables is limited to £2,000 per item unless the item is specified in the schedule. You must notify us of any change in your circumstances which may affect the cover provided under this policy.
