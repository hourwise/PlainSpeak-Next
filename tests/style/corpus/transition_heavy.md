# Assessment findings

The review examined three areas of the service. Furthermore, it considered the
experience of people using it. Furthermore, the team looked at the cost of each
route into the service.

Furthermore, waiting times were measured at four points in the year. The results
varied by region. Furthermore, the variation was larger than expected in two
areas.

Furthermore, staff reported difficulty with the current recording system. This
appeared in every interview. Furthermore, the same issue was raised in the
written responses.

Furthermore, the panel recommends a further review in twelve months. The
recommendations are set out below. Furthermore, the costs are given in the
appendix.
