# Scheme guidance

It is important to note that applications close at the end of March. Late
applications cannot be considered.

It is worth noting that the eligibility rules changed in January. The previous
guidance no longer applies.

It should be noted that supporting evidence must be supplied at the same time as
the form. Evidence sent later will not be attached.

It is important to note that a decision usually takes eight weeks. You will be
told in writing.

It is worth noting that there is no right of appeal against the funding
decision. You may reapply in the following year.

It should be noted that this guidance is reviewed annually. The next review is
due in the autumn.
