# Booking a repair

If something in your home stops working, tell us. You can call, or fill in the
form online — whichever is easier.

We'll ask what's broken and how urgent it is. That's usually enough. Sometimes
we need a photograph, especially for damp or a leak that isn't obvious, and
we'll say so at the time.

Most repairs happen within ten working days. Emergencies are different: if
there's no heating in winter, or water coming through a ceiling, someone should
be with you the same day. Say so when you call, and don't wait to see whether it
gets worse.

What if you're out when the engineer arrives? Ring us and we'll rebook. There's
no charge for the first missed visit.

One thing worth knowing. If the damage was caused by something you did — a nail
through a pipe, say — we may ask you to pay. We'll always tell you before any
work starts, and you can decide then.
