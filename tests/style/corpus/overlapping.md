# Annual report extract

The service handled forty thousand enquiries during the year. Most arrived by
telephone, although the proportion arriving online continued to grow. Waiting
times improved in three regions and worsened in one. Staff turnover remained
broadly stable across the whole period.

Funding was confirmed for a further two years in the autumn. This allowed the
team to recruit into two vacant posts that had been held open since the spring.

Training was delivered to every member of the assessment team. The programme
covered the revised eligibility rules and the new recording system, and was
delivered in four sessions across the year.

A new telephone system was installed in the second quarter. Early indications
suggest it has reduced the number of abandoned calls, though a full evaluation
has not yet been completed.
