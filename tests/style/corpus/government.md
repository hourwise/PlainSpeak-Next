# Housing benefit: how we work out your payment

We look at your income, your savings and your rent. We then compare these with
the amount the law says you need to live on.

If your income is at or below that amount, you may get help with all your
eligible rent. If it is above, we reduce the payment by 65 pence for every extra
pound you have.

Some income is ignored. This includes the first £5 of earnings if you are
single, and any disability living allowance you receive. Child benefit does not
count either.

Savings matter above £6,000. Between £6,000 and £16,000 we treat every £250 as
though it produced £1 a week in income. Above £16,000 you cannot usually claim.

Tell us within one month if anything changes. If you tell us late and we have
paid you too much, you will normally have to pay it back.
