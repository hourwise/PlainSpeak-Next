PATIENT DISCHARGE INSTRUCTIONS

POST-OPERATIVE CARE FOLLOWING LAPAROSCOPIC CHOLECYSTECTOMY

You have undergone a laparoscopic cholecystectomy, which is the surgical removal of your gallbladder using minimally invasive techniques. Please adhere to the following postoperative instructions to facilitate optimal recovery and minimize the probability of postoperative complications.

ACTIVITY RESTRICTIONS

Ambulation is encouraged to reduce the risk of deep vein thrombosis and pulmonary complications. However, strenuous physical exertion, including heavy lifting exceeding ten pounds, should be avoided for a period of four to six weeks postoperatively. You may resume activities of daily living as tolerated.

INCISION CARE

Your surgical incisions are covered with sterile adhesive dressings. These dressings should be maintained in a clean and dry condition. You may shower 48 hours after your procedure, but immersion in water (bathing, swimming, hot tub utilization) is contraindicated until your incisions have achieved complete healing. Monitor your incisions for signs of infection including erythema, purulent drainage, increasing tenderness, or febrile episodes.

MEDICATION REGIMEN

You have been prescribed analgesic medication for pain management. Take your pain medication as directed. Narcotic analgesics may cause constipation; therefore, adequate hydration and dietary fiber intake are recommended. A stool softener may be utilized if necessary.

You should also complete the full course of any antibiotic prophylaxis that has been prescribed, even in the absence of symptomatology suggesting infection.

DIETARY GUIDELINES

Commence with clear liquids and advance to a regular diet as tolerated. Some patients experience temporary gastrointestinal disturbances including nausea, bloating, and diarrhea. Consuming smaller, more frequent meals may ameliorate these symptoms. Fatty food consumption should be limited initially, as your body is adapting to the absence of a gallbladder for bile storage.

WARNING SIGNS — SEEK IMMEDIATE MEDICAL ATTENTION

Present to the Emergency Department or contact your surgeon forthwith if you experience any of the following:

- Persistent or increasing abdominal pain unrelieved by analgesic medication
- Febrile temperature exceeding 101 degrees Fahrenheit
- Persistent nausea and vomiting precluding oral hydration
- Abdominal distension or rigidity
- Jaundice (yellowing of the skin or sclera)
- Dyspnea or chest pain
- Erythema, warmth, or purulent drainage from your incision sites

FOLLOW-UP APPOINTMENT

A postoperative follow-up appointment has been scheduled for you. It is imperative that you attend this appointment for wound assessment and to ensure your convalescence is progressing satisfactorily. Please contact our office if you need to reschedule.

If you have questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact our office during regular business hours. For after-hours emergencies, proceed to the nearest Emergency Department.
